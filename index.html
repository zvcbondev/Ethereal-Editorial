Beta reading FAQ

Can I use readers I already know?

Yes. Invite them by email or private link. Only people you invite or approve can read.

Can you help me find new readers?

List your book publicly and readers can request to join. You approve each one; readers aren't guaranteed.

How do I become a beta reader?

Apply on the sign-up page. Every reader is reviewed by Ethereal Editorial, and you get access to manuscripts once you're approved.

Can other authors see or comment on my book?

No. Authors can only see their own manuscripts. Only approved readers you've accepted can read and comment on your book.

Do readers pay anything?

Never. Reading is always free.

Editing FAQ

Is the sample edit really free?

Yes. Up to 1,000 words, no payment details, no obligation to continue.

How do I receive my edit?

As a Word file with tracked changes and comments, so you can accept or reject each change.

How do payments work?

Once you choose a package, we agree on payment with you in your private Ethereal Editorial chat. We only ever share payment details inside that chat, so never pay details sent to you by email, Facebook or anywhere else.

Is my manuscript kept private?

Always. Your work is never shared, published or used for anything beyond your edit.
