Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026

1. Who we are

Ethereal Editorial ("we", "us") runs a beta reading platform and book editing service. It is operated by Mustopha Ibrahim, based in Lagos, Nigeria. For anything about your data, email etherealeditorial2@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, password (stored encrypted), and whether you're an author or reader.
  • Reader applications: country, reading habits, experience, genres, an optional reading profile link, and why you want to beta read.
  • Your writing: manuscripts, sample chapters, book details, and questions you set for readers.
  • Feedback: comments, reactions, survey answers and reading progress.
  • Messages: anything you send in chat, including payment arrangements.
  • Payment records: the amount, date, reference and name on the sending account. We never see or store card numbers.
  • Technical data: device, browser, IP address and basic usage logs, used to keep the site secure and working.

3. How we use it

  • To run your account and show you the right features.
  • To review reader applications and keep the platform safe.
  • To share a manuscript only with readers its author has accepted.
  • To deliver sample edits and paid editing work.
  • To send you emails about your account, requests, feedback and orders.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse and security problems.

We rely on: providing the service you signed up for; our legitimate interest in keeping the platform safe and working; and your consent for anything optional, like marketing emails, which you can withdraw any time.

4. Your manuscript is yours

We never sell your manuscript, never share it with anyone you haven't accepted, and never use it to train AI models.

5. Who else sees your data

  • Other users: readers see only the manuscripts they've been accepted to. Authors see the comments, answers and profiles of their own readers.
  • Service providers: companies that host our site, database and files, and send our emails. They may only use your data to provide their service to us.
  • The law: if we are legally required to.

We don't sell your personal data, ever.

6. International transfers

We are based in Nigeria and our providers may store data in other countries. When data leaves the UK, EU or Nigeria, we use providers that offer appropriate safeguards for it.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. If you delete your account, we delete your manuscripts within 30 days and your other personal data within 90 days, except records we must keep for legal or accounting reasons, such as payment records.

8. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how we use it. Email us and we'll reply within 30 days. If you're unhappy with our response, you can complain to your local data protection authority: the ICO in the UK, your national authority in the EU, or the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

9. Security

We use encrypted connections, encrypted passwords and access controls so only the right people see each manuscript. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

10. Age

You must be at least 18 to use Ethereal Editorial.

11. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and secure. We don't use advertising cookies.

12. Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we'll email you before the change takes effect.

Terms of Service

Effective 15 October 2026

1. The service

Ethereal Editorial provides a beta reading platform, where authors share manuscripts with readers they approve, and a professional book editing service. By creating an account you agree to these terms and to our Privacy Policy.

2. Accounts

  • You must be at least 18 and give accurate details when you sign up.
  • Authors get instant access. Readers can sign in straight away but only see manuscripts once Ethereal Editorial approves their application.
  • You're responsible for keeping your password safe and for activity on your account.

3. Your work

You keep all rights to your manuscript. You give us permission to store it and show it only to readers you accept, and to our editors when you order an edit. We never publish, sell or use your work to train AI models.

4. Readers

  • Readers agree to the Reader Confidentiality rules for every book they read.
  • Feedback should be honest, specific and respectful. Harassment or abusive comments lead to removal.
  • Reading is always free. Readers are never asked to pay.

5. Editing orders

  • The free sample edit covers up to 1,000 words with no obligation.
  • Prices are per 1,000 words in US dollars. Your exact quote is confirmed after your sample edit.
  • Your slot is booked once your deposit is confirmed. The balance is due on delivery unless you paid in full.
  • Payment details are only ever shared inside your private Ethereal Editorial chat. Never pay details sent by email, Facebook or anywhere else.
  • If you cancel before work starts, your deposit is refunded minus any transfer fees. Once work has started, the deposit covers the work done.

6. Acceptable use

Don't upload work you don't have the right to share, don't copy or redistribute other authors' manuscripts, and don't try to access accounts or books that aren't yours.

7. Ending your account

You can delete your account any time. We may suspend accounts that break these terms. Data is then handled as described in our Privacy Policy.

8. Liability

We work carefully, but editing and reader feedback are opinions, not guarantees of publication or sales. To the extent the law allows, our liability is limited to the amount you paid for the service in question.

9. Contact

Questions about these terms: etherealeditorial2@gmail.com.

Reader Confidentiality

Effective 15 October 2026

Authors trust you with unpublished work. Every reader agrees to these rules before requesting a book.

What you agree to

  • Keep every manuscript confidential. Don't share, quote, screenshot or discuss its content outside Ethereal Editorial.
  • Don't copy, download or save any part of a manuscript. Copying and downloading are turned off in the reader for this reason.
  • Your comments are visible only to you and the author.
  • Aim to finish within the time the author sets, or let them know if you can't.
  • Don't post reviews of an unpublished book anywhere without the author's permission.

If something goes wrong

Breaking these rules means losing your reader account immediately. Authors can report a concern any time by emailing etherealeditorial2@gmail.com.
