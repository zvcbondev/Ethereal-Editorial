Access rules

  • Authors: instant access. They see and manage only their own manuscripts and comments.
  • Readers (pending): can log in but see no manuscripts until approved.
  • Readers (approved): can request books and comment only on books they've been accepted to read.
  • Authors cannot comment on another author's manuscript, even if they also hold a reader account.
